## Bright Star Mileage Log
* Expense Period Table
    * 2 columns, From and To (date range)
* Entry table
    * 5 columns
        * Date
        * Reason for Travel
        * Start Location
        * End Location
        * Miles traveled
    * table footer rows
        * total mileage
        * mileage rate
        * reimbursement (milage * rate)
* Comments section below table
* Signature and date lines
    * employee
    * approval

## Bright Star Time Sheet
* One week of time entries
    * Monday-Sunday
* Week ending date at top (last day of timesheet week)
    * date of the sunday at end of timesheet week
    * ex. week 11/9/26-11/15/26, week ending date = 11/15/26
* Client information section, single col table (each row new entry)
    * Name
    * Address
    * City, State, Zip
    * Phone
    * Service
    * Mileage
    * Name and Signature rows to right
* Entry table, 5 columns
    * Day & Date (ex. Monday 9/14/26)
    * Start (24hr time)
    * End (24hr time)
    * Total
    * Notes
